Upgrades

The upgrades are split into four pages.

Allegiance UpgradesThe treaty tiers and the twelve faction upgrades of every faction, the Proofs, Heritages, unique buildings, Bloodlines and Lineages. Pick a base faction to see its upgrades together with those of a prestige and an astral faction. Magic UpgradesThe Magic upgrades, including the spell tiers: one stack per spell with its tiers 2 to 5 and what unlocks each. Secret UpgradesThe Secret upgrades: the ones that appear once you own a trophy or an artifact. Miscellaneous UpgradesThe Misc upgrades (the Gem, Reincarnation, Ruby and Challenge powers and the clicking, offline and assistant series) and the Building upgrades.
