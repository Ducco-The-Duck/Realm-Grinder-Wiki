Factions

Factions are the peoples you can side with. Each faction has its own Faction Coins, unique buildings and upgrades. You start with the six vanilla factions, and more become available as you progress.

Ascension penalty. From Ascension 1 on, the game reduces the percentage effects of many upgrades. If an upgrade's value is v (in %), the effect you get is , where k is the number of A marks on the upgrade: A is k = 1 (exponent 0.1), AA is k = 2 (exponent 0.01), AAA is k = 3 (exponent 0.001), and so on, so more As means a harsher penalty. The marks follow the Ascension chosen below. Most are reduced from Ascension 1, so k equals the Ascension number; some start later and have fewer As, or none, at a given Ascension. Upgrades without a mark are not reduced. The formulas in the hover popups are always the base values v.
