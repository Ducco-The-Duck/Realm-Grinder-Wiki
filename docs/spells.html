Spells

Spells are powers you cast with mana. Each spell has a mana cost, which is taken from your mana when you cast it, and most last for a limited time, during which their effect is active. A spell that is still active can't be cast again until it ends.

Pseudo-Faction spells count as Faction spells for stats, but are not affected by effects that affect Faction spells.
