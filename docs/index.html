Realm Grinder is an idle fantasy game: you build up a kingdom, choose a faction, cast spells, dig for artifacts and complete challenges, then reincarnate to start over with permanent bonuses and Ascensions that take the game further.

This wiki is under development. The Codex in particular is still being written and is not complete yet.

An unofficial fan wiki for Realm Grinder, not affiliated with or endorsed by the game's developers. Realm Grinder and everything in it belongs to its owners: all the art on this wiki (icons, frames and fonts) is taken from the game's files and is not owned by the wiki's author.

The numbers, formulas and requirements are read from the game's own code (version 4.3.15) and checked against the game as we go. If something looks wrong, or you have a suggestion, please share it on GitHub.
