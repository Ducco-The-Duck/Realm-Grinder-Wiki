Research

264 researches in 7 branches, in the in-game order (by research number). Hover a research for what it does, its formula and its requirements. The large icon is where you buy research points for the branch.

Ascension penalty. From Ascension 1 on, the game reduces most percentage effects. If a research's formula gives v (in %), the effect you get is , where k is the number of A marks on the research: A is k = 1 (exponent 0.1), AA is k = 2 (exponent 0.01), AAA is k = 3 (exponent 0.001), and so on, so more As means a harsher penalty. The marks follow the Ascension chosen below. Most researches are reduced from Ascension 1, so k equals the Ascension number; a few start later and have fewer As, or none, at a given Ascension. Researches without a mark are not reduced. The formulas in the hover popups are always the base values v.
The A marks and the greyed-out researches (not available in it) follow the chosen Ascension.
