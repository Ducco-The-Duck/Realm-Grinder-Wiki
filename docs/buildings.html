Buildings

Buildings are bought with coins and produce coins every second. The price of each kind rises with every one you own.

Ascension penalty. From Ascension 1 on, the game reduces the percentage effects of many upgrades. If an upgrade's value is v (in %), the effect you get is , where k is the number of A marks on the upgrade: A is k = 1 (exponent 0.1), AA is k = 2 (exponent 0.01), AAA is k = 3 (exponent 0.001), and so on, so more As means a harsher penalty. The marks follow the Ascension chosen below. Most are reduced from Ascension 1, so k equals the Ascension number; some start later and have fewer As, or none, at a given Ascension. Upgrades without a mark are not reduced. The formulas shown are always the base values v.
